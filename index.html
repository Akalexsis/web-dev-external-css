Why use this app

This app was created for university students. It is meant to help you find meals that align with your prefrences and your grocery budget. All you have to do is tell the app what type of cuisine you like to cook, share your grocery budget, and let the app help you plan ahead! So no more worrying about where your next meal will come from!

I created this app because it is difficult to plan meals as a college student. As a student, I have a limited grocery budget, but I still want to make my own meals. Getting a meal plan is too expensive and I'd like to make meals that I'll actually enjoy. But, I don't want to sacrifice my budget to make a delicious meal. So, this app helps students create grocery lists that align with their budget and their food prefrences.

Overall, my goal is to ensure each student has access to quality food choices. Not everyone can afford to eat out regularly. Whether it's because they physically can't dine out, can't afford to, or they simply just don't want to, everyone should have a choice on how they feed themselves.
